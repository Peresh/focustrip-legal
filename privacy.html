Focus Trip — Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-11-17

Focus Trip 将入住记录、仪式、目标与偏好保存在本机。Apple 登录可选择使用；App 在设备钥匙串保存 Apple 用户标识，并可能将 Apple 提供的姓名填入你可修改的个人资料。App 不保存 Apple 登录返回的邮箱，也不会取得 Apple 账户密码。符合条件的会员可通过 Apple 的 iCloud 私有数据库同步个人资料、所选头像、入住记录、仪式、目标、旅程收藏与部分偏好。Apple 登录与 iCloud 相互独立。

定位权限可选择开启，并可在设置中管理；此版本不读取或上传设备坐标。通知只用于你开启的提醒。会员付款由 Apple 处理，Focus Trip 不获取付款资料。

你可在数据管理中导出、导入或清除本机数据，并单独删除此 App 的 iCloud 数据。退出登录不会删除任一份数据。账户与安全也可删除保存的身份与本机数据，并由你选择是否同时删除独立的 iCloud 副本。删除后请按 App 内说明，到系统设置停止使用 Apple 登录。移除 App 会删除本机文件（设备备份除外），但钥匙串中的登录标识可能保留；如需一并移除，请卸载前使用账户与安全中的删除功能。iCloud 数据会保留，直到你主动删除。

反馈入口会打开邮件 App 中可编辑的草稿，不会自动发送；发送后，你选择的反馈内容和发件地址会送达支持邮箱。如有疑问，请发送邮件至 pereshcheng@gmail.com。

Focus Trip keeps stays, rituals, goals and preferences on your device. Apple sign-in is optional; the app stores your Apple user identifier in the device Keychain and may use the name Apple provides as your editable profile name. The app does not save the email returned by Apple sign-in and does not receive your Apple Account password. Eligible members may sync their profile, chosen avatar, stays, rituals, goals, journey collections and selected preferences through Apple's private iCloud database. Apple sign-in and iCloud are independent.

Location permission is optional and can be managed in Settings. This version does not read or upload device coordinates. Notifications are used only for reminders you enable. Apple processes membership payments; Focus Trip does not receive payment details.

Data Controls lets you export, import or erase local data and separately delete this app's iCloud data. Signing out does not erase either copy. Account & Security also lets you delete the saved identity and local data, with a separate choice to delete the independent iCloud copy. After deletion, follow the in-app instructions to stop using Sign in with Apple in system settings. Removing the app removes its local files unless included in a device backup. Keychain sign-in information may remain after uninstalling; use Account & Security before uninstalling to remove it. iCloud data remains until you delete it.

Sending feedback opens your email app with an editable draft; nothing is sent automatically. Your chosen message and sender address reach our support mailbox only when you send it. For questions, email pereshcheng@gmail.com.


手机号登录补充 / Phone sign-in addendum (2026-11-16)
手机号与验证码会发送至 Focus Trip 认证服务，由短信服务商投递请求的验证码。认证服务保存手机号账户与会话记录；设备钥匙串保存会话凭据和脱敏手机号。手机号登录不会上传旅程记录。账户与安全可删除已登录的手机号账户，服务不可用时需重试。国际短信与 Google 登录尚未接通。
Phone sign-in sends the number and verification code to the Focus Trip authentication service; the SMS provider delivers requested codes. The service stores phone accounts and sessions; the device Keychain stores the session token and masked number. Phone sign-in does not upload journey records. Account & Security deletes the signed-in phone account; retry if the service is unavailable. International SMS and Google sign-in are not connected.
