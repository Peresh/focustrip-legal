Focus Trip — Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-11-17

运营主体与联系：Focus Trip 由上海柯伊伯带艺术设计有限责任公司提供。本政策说明你使用 App 时个人信息的用途、接收方和管理方式。隐私问题或个人信息权利申请可发至 pereshcheng@gmail.com。你可以不登录，使用基础入住和本机记录等功能；可选服务仅在你选择使用时处理相应信息。

Focus Trip 将入住记录、仪式、目标与偏好保存在本机。Apple 登录可选择使用；App 在设备钥匙串保存 Apple 用户标识，并可能将 Apple 提供的姓名填入你可修改的个人资料。App 不保存 Apple 登录返回的邮箱，也不会取得 Apple 账户密码。符合条件的会员可通过 Apple 的 iCloud 私有数据库同步个人资料、所选头像、入住记录、仪式、目标、旅程收藏与部分偏好。Apple 登录与 iCloud 相互独立。

手机号登录会将手机号和验证码发送至 Focus Trip 认证服务。服务通过阿里云号码认证服务向该手机号发送验证码，并保存手机号账户、登录会话及用于防止滥用的记录；会话还包含请求的客户端信息。设备钥匙串保存会话凭据和脱敏手机号。手机号登录不会将旅程记录上传到认证服务，也不会自动合并 Apple 登录、iCloud 或购买身份。你可在账户与安全中删除已登录的手机号账户；服务不可用或会话失效时，需恢复服务或重新登录后重试。国际短信与 Google 登录尚未接通。

Focus Trip 使用 Apple WeatherKit 获取所选场景固定地点的天气，并在设备上缓存结果。发送给 Apple 的是场景经纬度和天气查询所需的时间范围，不是手机的当前位置。本版本的定位入口仅管理系统授权状态，不读取或上传设备坐标。通知只用于你开启的本机提醒。

Apple 处理会员付款。Focus Trip 在设备上读取商品和经过 Apple 验证的购买、到期及撤销状态，以提供和恢复会员权益；不会取得你的银行卡或付款账户资料，也不会把这些交易上传到手机号认证服务。

你可在数据管理中导出、导入或清除本机数据，并单独删除此 App 的 iCloud 数据。退出登录不会删除任一份数据。账户与安全也可删除保存的身份与本机数据，并由你选择是否同时删除独立的 iCloud 副本。删除后请按 App 内说明，到系统设置停止使用 Apple 登录。移除 App 会删除本机文件（设备备份除外），但钥匙串中的登录标识可能保留；如需一并移除，请卸载前使用账户与安全中的删除功能。iCloud 数据会保留，直到你主动删除。

数据管理导出的备份可能包含个人资料、头像、入住记录、目标、仪式和偏好。导出仅准备文件，你通过系统界面选择保存或分享的位置，不会自动发送给我们。反馈可通过邮件或系统分享发送；草稿会包含你填写的内容和联系方式，只有你确认发送后，接收方才会收到这些内容及相应发件信息。本版本不会自动附加旅程数据库或诊断日志。

客服邮件与境外处理：客服邮箱使用 Google LLC 及其适用关联公司提供的 Gmail，用于接收、处理和回复你主动发送的来信。Google 可能在中国大陆以外处理邮件内容、你提供的联系方式及发件信息。App 会在打开客服邮件前单独提示并征求你的同意；你仍可检查草稿、取消或自行发送。不开启邮件不会影响基础使用，也不会自动发送反馈或旅程数据。若使用系统分享，接收方和发送内容由你选择。Google 的隐私政策及联系途径见下方链接；有关我们保存的客服邮件，可向上述邮箱申请查阅、更正或删除。

服务提供方：Apple 提供 Apple 登录、iCloud、WeatherKit 和 App Store 购买服务，分别处理上述登录信息、你选择同步的内容、场景天气请求及购买信息；这些服务受 Apple 对应政策约束。中国大陆 iCloud 由云上贵州（云上艾珀（贵州）技术有限公司）运营，适用的云数据存储在中国大陆；其他 Apple 服务的处理地区以对应政策为准。阿里云计算有限公司提供中国大陆认证服务器；阿里巴巴云计算（北京）有限公司提供号码认证服务，处理我们为发送短信提交的手机号、验证码和短信参数。验证码由 Focus Trip 认证服务校验。本 App 通过自己的认证服务调用短信接口，未接入阿里云一键登录客户端 SDK，不因该服务读取 SIM 卡信息。各提供方的政策链接列在本页下方。

保存与删除：本机数据及你选择的 iCloud 副本按前述方式由你管理和删除。手机号账户在你使用期间保留，注销时从在线账户库删除并撤销关联会话；这不等于所有存储介质即时物理擦除。登录会话按实际有效期失效，已过期的会话、验证码及限流记录每日清理，不把有效期等同于物理删除时间。为维护服务安全，相关安全日志记录请求时间、来源 IP、操作类型和结果，保存至少六个自然月；正常按月轮换通常保存七至八个月，以避免轮换日期变化导致不足六个月；停机等异常可能延后清理，依法需要继续保存的必要记录除外。客服邮件在问题处理及必要后续联系结束后删除；有未解决的争议或法定保留义务时，仅继续保存所必需的内容。我们暂不另建手机号账户库的周期性备份；账号库故障时可能需要重新验证手机号，此库不保存旅程或会员数据。你自行导出的文件、设备备份及服务商依法保留的记录按相应位置和服务规则管理；删除 App 内数据不会自动删除你已分享或另存的文件。

你的权利：你可在 App 修改个人资料、导出或删除本机数据，并可另行删除此 App 的 iCloud 副本、关闭同步或在系统设置撤回可选权限。需要查阅、复制、更正或删除认证服务中的个人信息，或对处理方式提出限制、撤回同意、解释或投诉请求时，请联系上述邮箱并说明涉及的功能。为防止他人冒用，我们会核验与你的请求相称的必要身份信息；请勿通过邮件发送密码、短信验证码、身份证件或完整旅程备份。无法按请求处理时会说明原因和进一步联系途径。撤回可选服务的授权不影响此前已合法进行的处理，也不妨碍你继续使用不依赖该信息的基础功能。

未成年人信息与政策更新：如监护人认为我们收到了不应处理的未成年人个人信息，可通过上述邮箱联系，申请核实或删除。请勿在反馈中发送未成年人的敏感信息。政策内容如有实质变化，我们会在 App 或政策页面提示；新的处理需要你的同意时，会另行征求。

You can use core stays and local records without signing in; optional services process the information needed when you choose to use them.

Focus Trip keeps stays, rituals, goals and preferences on your device. Apple sign-in is optional; the app stores your Apple user identifier in the device Keychain and may use the name Apple provides as your editable profile name. The app does not save the email returned by Apple sign-in and does not receive your Apple Account password. Eligible members may sync their profile, chosen avatar, stays, rituals, goals, journey collections and selected preferences through Apple's private iCloud database. Apple sign-in and iCloud are independent.

Phone sign-in sends your phone number and verification code to the Focus Trip authentication service. The service uses Alibaba Cloud Phone Number Verification Service to deliver the requested code and stores phone accounts, sign-in sessions and records used to prevent abuse. Sessions also include request client information. Your device Keychain stores the session token and masked number. Phone sign-in does not upload journey records to the authentication service or automatically merge Apple sign-in, iCloud or purchase identities. You can delete the signed-in phone account in Account & Security; retry after service recovery or signing in again if the service is unavailable or the session has expired. International SMS and Google sign-in are not connected.

Focus Trip uses Apple WeatherKit to request weather for the fixed location of a selected scene and caches the results on your device. Requests contain the scene coordinates and the time range needed for the forecast, not your phone’s current location. This version’s location control only manages system permission and does not read or upload device coordinates. Notifications are used only for local reminders you enable.

Apple processes membership payments. Focus Trip reads products and Apple-verified purchase, expiration and revocation status on your device to provide and restore membership access. It does not receive your card or payment account details or upload these transactions to the phone authentication service.

Data Controls lets you export, import or erase local data and separately delete this app's iCloud data. Signing out does not erase either copy. Account & Security also lets you delete the saved identity and local data, with a separate choice to delete the independent iCloud copy. After deletion, follow the in-app instructions to stop using Sign in with Apple in system settings. Removing the app removes its local files unless included in a device backup. Keychain sign-in information may remain after uninstalling; use Account & Security before uninstalling to remove it. iCloud data remains until you delete it.

Data Controls can export a backup containing your profile, avatar, stays, goals, rituals and preferences. Export only prepares a file; you choose where to save or share it through the system interface, and it is not automatically sent to us. Feedback can be sent through email or system sharing. The draft includes the content and contact information you enter. The recipient receives that content and the relevant sender information only when you confirm sending. This version does not automatically attach your journey database or diagnostic logs.

Retention and deletion: You manage and delete local data and your selected iCloud copy as described above. Phone accounts remain while you use them; account deletion removes the account from the live account database and revokes its sessions. This does not mean every storage medium is immediately physically erased. Sign-in sessions expire according to their actual validity, and expired sessions, verification codes and rate-limit records are cleared daily; expiry is not the same as physical deletion. Relevant security logs record request time, source IP address, operation type and outcome and are retained for at least six calendar months. Normal monthly rotation usually retains records for seven to eight months to avoid falling below six months when rotation dates vary; downtime or other disruptions may delay cleanup, except where continued retention of necessary records is required by law. Support email is deleted when the issue and necessary follow-up are complete; only necessary content is retained for unresolved disputes or legal obligations. We do not currently create separate periodic backups of the phone account database. If that database fails, you may need to verify your number again; it contains no journey or membership data. Files you export, device backups and records that providers must retain are managed under the rules of their respective locations and services. Deleting data in the app does not automatically remove files you have shared or saved elsewhere.
