需要帮助？

请发邮件至 pereshcheng@gmail.com。也可使用 App 内的反馈入口，检查邮件草稿后自行发送。请说明 App 版本、设备与系统版本、问题发生时间、操作步骤和错误提示；不要发送密码、短信验证码或完整付款资料。

恢复购买

  1. 确认 App Store 使用的是当初购买会员的 Apple 账户，然后打开 Focus Trip 的会员页，选择“恢复购买”。
  2. 用于 App Store 付款的 Apple 账户与 App 内“通过 Apple 登录”是不同用途；在 App 内重新登录并不能替代恢复购买。若提示未找到有效会员，请先核对 Apple 购买记录和订阅状态，避免重复购买。

恢复购买用于找回仍有效的订阅或已购买的终身权益，不会恢复入住历史。参阅 Apple 的恢复购买说明；仍有问题时请联系我们。

取消订阅与终身会员

在设备“设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅”中找到 Focus Trip，按 Apple 显示的选项取消续订。也可打开 管理订阅。具体到期时间以 Apple 显示为准。

终身会员为一次性购买，不自动续订。购买终身会员不会自动取消已有的月度或年度订阅，请另行管理。不使用 App、退出登录、删除数据或卸载 App，都不会自动取消订阅。参阅 Apple 的取消订阅说明。

导出、导入与旧测试版迁移

在 App 的“数据管理”中选择“导出数据”，将文件保存到你能找到的位置。需要转移时，在目标版本的“数据管理”中选择“导入数据”，按结果提示核对记录；不要通过修改导出文件来绕过错误。

如果旧测试版与新版本使用不同的安装标识（Bundle ID，系统用它区分 App），它们会分别安装，旧记录不会自动转入新版本。请保留旧 App，先从旧版导出，再在新版本导入；核对入住记录、目标、仪式和个人资料后，再决定是否移除旧版。导入前，也请导出目标版本已有的数据。

如果旧版没有导出入口，或新版本提示格式不支持、文件损坏或数据冲突，请保留两个 App 和原导出文件，联系我们。登录相同 Apple 账户或手机号不会自动合并两份本机数据；不要为了导入而先删除现有记录。

退出登录、删除账户和删除数据

手机号登录、App 内 Apple 登录、iCloud 和 App Store 购买相互独立。退出登录保留本机记录，也不会取消订阅。

  • 本机数据：“数据管理 → 删除所有本机数据”用于清除这台设备上的 App 数据。删除前，请先导出要保留的内容；这不等于删除独立的 iCloud 副本。
  • 手机号账户：“账户与安全”的账户删除流程会删除当前已登录的手机号账户及其会话。服务不可用时需要重试，不要将错误提示当作删除成功。手机号登录本身不会上传旅程记录。
  • Apple 登录：“账户与安全”可移除 App 保存的本机身份。删除后，请按 App 内说明，在系统设置中停止对 Focus Trip 使用“通过 Apple 登录”；这不会删除你的 Apple 账户。参阅 Apple 的授权管理说明。
  • iCloud 副本：可在“数据管理”单独删除此 App 的 iCloud 数据，或在“账户与安全”删除时选择同时删除。操作针对当前核实的 iCloud 账户；单独删除云端副本会保留本机数据并关闭自动同步。若提示账户不符或删除未完成，请先保留本机数据并按提示处理；其他设备也应先关闭同步。

“账户与安全”的整体删除还会清除本机数据；请在确认前检查是否勾选了 iCloud。卸载 App 不等于删除手机号账户、撤销 Apple 授权或清除 iCloud 数据。我们不能通过客服邮件直接读取或代为找回你的 iCloud 私有记录。

数据处理范围与使用条件见 隐私政策 和 使用条款。

Need help?

Email pereshcheng@gmail.com, or use the feedback option in the app and send the draft after reviewing it. Include your app version, device and OS version, when the issue happened, the steps you took and the error message. Do not send passwords, verification codes or full payment details.

Restore purchases

  1. Make sure the App Store uses the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open the membership page in Focus Trip and select Restore Purchases.
  2. The Apple Account used for App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple inside the app serve different purposes. Signing in again does not replace restoring purchases. If no active membership is found, check your Apple purchase history and subscription status before buying again.

Restoring purchases can recover an active subscription or a purchased lifetime entitlement. It does not restore your stay history. See Apple's purchase restoration guide, or contact us if you still need help.

Cancel a subscription or manage lifetime membership

On your device, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select Focus Trip and follow Apple's cancellation options. You can also open Manage Subscriptions. Check Apple for the applicable expiration date.

Lifetime membership is a one-time purchase and does not renew automatically. Buying lifetime membership does not cancel an existing monthly or yearly subscription. Manage that subscription separately. Not using the app, signing out, deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. See Apple's cancellation guide.

Export, import and move from an older test app

Open Data Controls in the app, select Export Data and save the file somewhere you can find it. To move the data, open Data Controls in the destination app, select Import Data and check the result. Do not edit an export file to bypass an error.

If an older test app and a newer version have different installation identifiers (Bundle IDs, which the system uses to distinguish apps), they install separately. Old records do not move automatically. Keep the old app, export from it, then import into the newer app. Check your stays, goals, rituals and profile before deciding whether to remove the old app. Also export any existing data from the destination app before importing.

If the old app has no export option, or the new app reports an unsupported format, damaged file or data conflict, keep both apps and the original export and contact us. Using the same Apple Account or phone number does not automatically merge their local data. Do not delete existing records to force an import.

Sign out, delete an account or delete data

Phone sign-in, Sign in with Apple, iCloud and App Store purchases are independent. Signing out keeps local records and does not cancel subscriptions.

  • Local data: Data Controls → Delete All Local Data removes app data on this device. Export anything you want to keep first. This does not delete the separate iCloud copy.
  • Phone account: Account & Security's account deletion flow deletes the currently signed-in phone account and its sessions. If the service is unavailable, retry; an error does not confirm deletion. Phone sign-in itself does not upload journey records.
  • Sign in with Apple: Account & Security can remove the identity saved by this app on your device. After deletion, follow the in-app instructions to stop using Sign in with Apple for Focus Trip in system settings. This does not delete your Apple Account. See Apple's authorization management guide.
  • iCloud copy: Use Data Controls to delete this app's iCloud data separately, or select that option during deletion in Account & Security. The operation uses the verified current iCloud account. Deleting only the cloud copy keeps local data and turns off automatic sync. If the app reports an account mismatch or incomplete deletion, keep your local data and follow its instructions. Turn off sync on your other devices first as well.

The full Account & Security deletion also removes local data. Check whether iCloud deletion is selected before confirming. Uninstalling the app does not delete a phone account, revoke Apple authorization or clear iCloud data. Support cannot directly read or retrieve your private iCloud records by email.
